What is this?

Simulates headcount month by month assuming no new hires — starting from the current closing HC (which already reflects actual separations this month), then deducting only forecasted attritions (Tentative + Confirmed) going forward. Rate % and annualized rate show how fast each dept is losing people. Click any count to view names.

HC hits zero?Hard hiring deadline — act before that month.
Annualized spikes?Attrition accelerating — escalate to leadership.
✓ Status flagsGreen <10% · Orange 10–20% · Red >20% drop.
Column guide: −Sep/−Attr = separations deducted  ·  Proj HC = running HC after deductions  ·  Rate % = Sep ÷ avg(start, end HC) × 100  ·  Ann % = Rate × 12  ·  Rate & Ann shown at dept level only.

Provider Dependency
HC concentration per provider —
Provider Dependency Index measures what % of each department's HC sits with a single provider. Departments above 80% concentration in one provider are flagged as high business continuity risk — a single provider issue would disproportionately impact that team.
IC Tenure Risk
Individual Contributors still ramping —
IC Tenure Risk shows what % of Individual Contributors are still within their ramp period (≤90 days). High % means a large proportion of your agent workforce is not yet fully productive — a risk for service quality and capacity planning. ≤30d = critical (just started), 30–60d = early ramp, 60–90d = late ramp.
